# 📈 Bright Bank Sales Trends Analysis (Dec 2013 - Nov 2016)
## 📜 Overview
This repository details a comprehensive analysis of Bright Bank's sales and profitability trends over a three-year period, from Dec 2013 to Nov 2016. The analysis explores how pricing strategies, daily and weekly performance, and promotional activities influenced both revenue and gross profit outcomes.
## 🎯 Aim
The goal of this analysis is to provide a comprehensive understanding of Bright Bank's sales performance and the impact of its pricing and promotional strategies. The analysis concludes with strategic recommendations aimed at improving profitability, pricing efficiency, and sustainable business growth.
## 💡 Key Insights
The analysis highlighted several critical findings regarding price fluctuation, profit trends, and promotional effectiveness:
 * Pricing Strategy & Growth:
   * The daily sales price per unit fluctuates significantly, confirming a high-low pricing strategy where the highest daily unit price recorded was R44 and the lowest was R31.
   * The average unit sales price has shown consistent year-over-year growth, reaching its highest annual average of R38 in 2016.
 * Gross Profit and Daily Performance:
   * Daily gross profit percentages have fluctuated significantly, hitting a high of 14.4% (in July 2016) and a low of -14.9% (in November 2015).
   * Saturday generated the highest revenue (R41,857,085.00) but also recorded the highest daily gross profit loss of R1,799,419.00, resulting in the worst daily gross profit margin of -4.3%.
   * Saturday also posted the lowest daily gross profit per unit (-1.5%), with Sunday following closely at -1.2%, indicating weaker weekend profitability.
 * Promotional Impact:
   * Promotional pricing demonstrates a strong impact on driving sales volume.
   * On a specific "First Date" promotion, the quantity sold (13,500 units at a promo price of R30.82) was more than twice the total normal quantity sold (6,338 units) over a three-day period.
   * However, promotional activity is associated with losses: the "Loss-Making Line" chart reveals a consistent negative gross profit trend during promotional periods, peaking at -11% on the "First Date".
## ✅ Recommendations
The following strategic recommendations are provided to improve profitability, pricing efficiency, and sustained growth:
 * Bundle Promotions to Drive Cross-Selling:
   * 🎁 Bundle high-volume promo items with high-margin or slow-moving products to recover lost profit and increase total transaction value.
 * Optimize Promotional Pricing Strategy:
   * 🏷️ Limit deep discounts and instead use value-based pricing to maintain profitability.
 * Improve Weekend Profitability:
   * 📉 Review cost structures and adjust weekend offers to maintain profitability while sustaining traffic.
 * Leverage Customer Insights for Targeted Offers:
   * 🎯 Personalize bundles and discounts based on customer buying patterns to improve conversion and margin.
